About Kilimo News

Kilimo News is a media and consulting company based in Nairobi, Kenya, specializing in agriculture, agribusiness, and development sectors. We connect international buyers directly to Kenya's coffee supply chain from licensed NCE dealers and certified brokers to estates and smallholder producers making it simple to source and purchase premium Kenyan coffee.

We develop content for stakeholders in the digital agriculture space in Kenya, serving as the thought partner of choice for private-sector corporations, governments, and development agents investing in agriculture in Africa. Our mission is to make agriculture investments in Africa attractive and rewarding to both private and public investors.
