Who We Are

About Kilimo News

Kilimo News is a media and consulting company based in Nairobi, Kenya, specializing in agriculture, agribusiness, and development sectors. We connect farmers, cooperatives, licensed dealers, and global roasters with verified trade opportunities.

By pairing on-the-ground reporting from Kenya's coffee-growing counties with live auction pricing data, we help stakeholders make informed buying, milling, and marketing decisions every season.

Our Vision

To be East Africa's premier digital hub for transparent agricultural market intelligence and coffee trade.

Our Mission

Empowering producers and buyers with timely market data, fair trade access, and actionable industry reporting.
